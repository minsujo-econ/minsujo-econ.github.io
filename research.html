Job Market Paper

Large Exporters, Industrial Allocation, and Foreign Demand Shocks

Using monthly Mexican firm-level export data, I show that large exporters experienced larger declines during the COVID-19 trade collapse because they were disproportionately represented in harder-hit industries, rather than because they were more responsive within industries. I then show that this industrial allocation of large exporters amplified the impact of U.S. demand shocks transmitted to Mexican exports through global input-output linkages.

The Impact of U.S. Monetary Policy on FDI between Non-U.S. Countries

A structural VAR shows that higher U.S. interest rates reduce Korean FDI in China, providing evidence that U.S. monetary policy affects FDI between non-U.S. countries.

Are External Vulnerabilities Affecting the Volatility of the Exchange Rate?

Master's Thesis

Across 32 countries, I find that short-term external banking liabilities increase exchange rate volatility in EGARCH estimates.
